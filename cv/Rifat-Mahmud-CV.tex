\project{Explainable Hybrid Ensemble Diagnosis of Five Lung Conditions}{ULAB thesis, 2026}
\begin{itemize}[label=$\circ$]
    \item Five-class chest X-ray system (bacterial/viral pneumonia, COVID-19, tuberculosis, normal) that segments the lungs and refuses a label when the upload fails the lung check.
    \item Ensemble of DenseNet201, ResNet50, VGG16, and EfficientNet-B3 fine-tuned on 35,686 lung-cropped images: 89.23\% accuracy and 91.67\% macro-F1 on 7,651 held-out images.
    \item Grad-CAM++, LIME, and SHAP explanations constrained to the lung mask.
    \item Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, PSPNet, CLAHE, Grad-CAM++, LIME, SHAP, Flask.
\end{itemize}
